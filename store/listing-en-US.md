# Microsoft Store listing (en-US)

## Name
TopBar Translate

## Short description
A lightweight translator that slides down when your mouse touches the top of the screen. Screenshot translation and offline voice input included.

## Description
TopBar Translate lives in the system tray. Move your mouse to the top-center of the screen and the translation panel slides down; move away and it tucks itself back.

• Hover to translate, or press Ctrl+Alt+T
• Automatic language detection (Chinese → English, everything else → Chinese, or pick your own)
• Screenshot translation: press Ctrl+Alt+S and select any area of the screen
• Offline voice input with a local Whisper model; recordings never leave your PC
• Free out of the box with Index-Translate, no sign-up
• Bring your own service: OpenAI, DeepSeek, Gemini, Claude, DeepL, Microsoft Translator, Ollama or a custom endpoint
• Searchable history with configurable retention
• Light and dark themes, interface in 9 languages

## Features
- Slide-down panel on top-edge hover
- Automatic language detection
- Region screenshot translation
- Offline voice input (local Whisper)
- Multiple translation providers and custom endpoints
- Searchable translation history
